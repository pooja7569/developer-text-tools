**Title:** Lead with the main task and one clear differentiator, for example
"JSON Formatter & Log Utility." Use "JSON Formatter" once, naturally. Keep the
name concise and distinctive. Avoid competitor names, repeated synonyms,
ranking claims, and keyword stuffing. Match the title to the shipped cloud
bundle rather than promising cloud snippet storage or background scraping.

**Tagline / item summary:** Write a benefit-first sentence under the store's
132-character summary limit, such as "Format JSON, capture selected text, and
convert application logs to CSV." Use "Developer Log Utility" as an alternative
natural phrase only if it improves clarity; do not cram every keyword into
every field. Match the summary to your screenshots and permission disclosures.

**Store description:** Open with the developer problem, then describe local
JSON formatting, click-to-capture selected text, and supported log-to-CSV
conversion. Follow with short feature bullets, three setup steps, supported
formats/limits, HTTPS data-transfer disclosures, and support/privacy links.
Use terms such as "JSON Formatter," "Developer Log Utility," and "CSV export"
only where they explain real functionality. Measure installs, retention,
uninstalls, and feedback after launch; improve onboarding and useful features.
Organic discovery depends on quality, demand, trust, and competition. No
keyword framework can guarantee thousands of users or growth on autopilot.
